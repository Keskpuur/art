About

Keskpuur is an artist-run gallery founded by artist and curator KitKit Para in 2023, he was interested in the history of the location, which is located inside a meat hall at Keskturg (central market) in Tallinn, and the current ongoing urban development of the neighbourhood. The plan is to operate the gallery until the market is demolished, which is expected to happen in the near future.

In spring and summer 2026, three new members joined the gallery: Senja Savtšenko, Éric-Olivier Thériault, and Kristen Punger.
